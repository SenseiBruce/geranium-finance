Harborview Media Group — FX and Period Reference
Treasury Policy Bulletin HM-FX-2025-02 | Effective Q1 2025
Owner: Treasury Operations | Review cycle: Quarterly

Reporting period
Consolidated management reporting for board materials uses calendar Q1 2025: January 1, 2025 through March 31, 2025. Monthly management packs may use alternate cutoffs; this bulletin governs the board consolidation package only.

Functional currency by label
- Beacon Media: USD (report directly to consolidated USD)
- Cascade Creative: CAD (convert to USD for consolidated view)
- Lattice Digital: USD

CAD/USD conversion
Use the Q1 2025 planning rate of 0.74 USD per CAD for all Cascade Creative revenue in consolidated reporting. Do not use month-end spot rates, Bloomberg screen rates, or payment-date rates for this board package. If Cascade submits invoices in CAD, translate using 0.74 unless treasury publishes a superseding bulletin.

Exclusions from consolidated Q1 revenue
Exclude ledger rows with status void, cancelled, or out_of_period from consolidated revenue. Rows dated before January 1, 2025 are out of scope for Q1 even if marked posted. The December 2024 Beacon catch-up row HM-4001 is an example of a row that must not roll into Q1 consolidated totals.

Reconciliation expectations
Controllers should document excluded rows in the workbook reconciliation section with transaction id, status, and dollar impact. Where the ledger mixes currencies, show both native currency and consolidated USD.
